## What Racing Drone is

**Racing Drone** is the mission repo of the Branes.AI platform: the whole
organism. [Cortex](https://branes-ai.github.io/cortex/) perceives the world and
plans where to fly, [Reflex](https://branes-ai.github.io/reflex/) makes the
airframe follow that path and keeps it safe, and
[Synapse](https://github.com/branes-ai/synapse) runs the learned models. This
repo owns the mission, wires them to a vehicle, and measures the result.

An autonomous racing drone is one of the hardest real-time embodied-AI problems
that fits on a desk. Every weakness in perception latency, state-estimation
accuracy, control bandwidth or compute efficiency shows up as lap time or as a
crash, which makes racing the platform's forcing function
([why racing](assessments/autonomous-drone-racing.md)).

## The platform at a glance

| Repo | Analogue | What it provides to the racing drone | Time scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`cortex`](https://github.com/branes-ai/cortex) | Cerebral cortex | Perception (VIO, gate detection, SLAM), world model, path planning | ~5–100 Hz |
| [`synapse`](https://github.com/branes-ai/synapse) | The synapses | Learned models as static, allocation-free compute graphs | Deterministic |
| [`reflex`](https://github.com/branes-ai/reflex) | Brainstem, spinal cord | Stabilization, trajectory tracking (PID → MPC), fault recovery, safety envelopes | ~100 Hz–8 kHz |
| **`racing-drone`** | The whole organism | Mission logic, vehicle integration, simulation and flight testing | — |
