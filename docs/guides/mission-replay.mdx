A SITL run writes a log of everything the stack saw and did. `trace_export`
turns the log into a replay in `reflex-trace/1`, the trace format of
[reflex](https://github.com/branes-ai/reflex), and this page plays it in
reflex's trace player, the same player reflex's own pages use. The player
runs nothing: it shows the logged truth sample by sample, with its provenance
underneath.

## Baseline B0, two laps

Baseline B0 (`b0-gt-pid`) flies a waypoint path through the gate centres
with the reflex position and attitude cascade, on perfect state. The run is
`scenarios/b0-nominal.yaml` at its own seed, 1700, on the
[A2RL 2025 course](../../courses/a2rl-2025.yaml). Its scoreboard row:
two laps complete, in 34.7 s and 34.0 s, at up to 4.5 m/s against the
scenario's 5 m/s limit.

What to look for:

- **The reference leads the vehicle.** The dashed line is the planner's
  reference, a point on the waypoint path through the gate centres. On the
  straights it runs 0.39 m ahead on average, a little more than the
  planner's 0.3 m lookahead. In the turns it runs 0.50 m ahead, where the
  path turns faster than the cascade follows.
- **The two laps look alike.** Lap 1 is timed from takeoff, at rest, and lap
  2 from the end of lap 1, at speed, so lap 2 is 0.7 s faster. Otherwise
  the path and the gate passages repeat.
- **The thrusts.** On the straights the four rotors stay within 0.02 N of
  each other, near a quarter of the vehicle's weight. In the turns they
  differ by about 1 N, as the cascade tilts the vehicle.

The view shows the vehicle, its trail, and its thrust; it does not draw the
course. The events step through the gate passages, and the overview camera
shows the whole path.

## Export a run

Build the tools and record a run, then export it:

```sh
cmake --preset gcc-release && cmake --build --preset gcc-release
build/gcc-release/apps/sitl_b0-gt-pid --scenario scenarios/b0-nominal.yaml --out run.brlog
build/gcc-release/apps/trace_export --log run.brlog --scenario scenarios/b0-nominal.yaml --out replay.json
```

- `--scenario` is the file the log was recorded from. It must hash to the
  log's `scenario_hash`, so the trace's provenance names the run's real
  inputs. A what-if replay, whose inputs were overridden, is refused.
- The trace samples the 1 kHz truth at `--rate`, 20 Hz by default. The rate's
  period must be a multiple of the physics step.
- The channels are the true `position`, `velocity`, `attitude`, `body_rates`,
  and `rotor_speeds`; `rotor_thrusts`, k_f·ω² with the vehicle's thrust
  coefficient; and `position_setpoint`, the planner's latest reference.
- The events are takeoff and every gate passage, from the same scorer as
  `score_log`. A run that completes the course ends 2 s after its last
  passage; any other run is kept to the end of its log.

`sim/replay_trace.hpp` documents the format's rules that the export keeps.

## Play it on a page

The player is the npm package `@branes-ai/trace-player`, which reflex
publishes with each release; `docs-site/package.json` pins it. In an `.mdx`
page under `docs/`, import the player and the trace, and give the player a
title that asks one question, a text description of what happens, and the
panels to plot:

```mdx
import TracePlayer from '@branes-ai/trace-player/TracePlayer.astro';
import nominal from '@site/traces/b0-nominal.json';

<TracePlayer trace={nominal} title="…?" description="…"
  plots={[{ channel: 'position', title: 'Position', labels: ['x', 'y', 'z'], setpoint: true, setpointLabel: 'reference' }]}
  repo="https://github.com/branes-ai/racing-drone" provenanceNote="a recorded SITL run" />
```

`repo` points the footer's links at this repository, and `provenanceNote`
says how the trace is kept; both default to reflex's. The site's build
validates the trace and fails on an invalid one. The package's README lists
the player's options.

This page's replay is recorded by `tools/gen_replay.sh`, which runs the SITL
and the export and stamps the trace with the commit of the tree. Rerun it
when a change to the stack changes the run, and commit the trace with the
change. Unlike reflex's traces, CI does not regenerate it. It is a record of
one run at one commit, and the provenance under the player names that commit.
